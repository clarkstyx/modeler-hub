Privacy Policy: Valencia Holdings

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Valencia Holdings ("we", "us") designs 3D printing files and sells them on Etsy. This policy explains what

information our tools handle, including our Pinterest app "Valencia Holdings Pin Scheduler".

Our Pinterest app

  • The app is used only by Valencia Holdings to publish Pins of our own designs to our own Pinterest business account, linking to our own Etsy listings.
  • It reads only our own boards and Pins, so it can choose where a new Pin goes.
  • It does not collect, store or share any information about other Pinterest users.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted by our connector service and used only to post to our account. We can revoke access at any time in Pinterest's settings.

Etsy customers

  • Orders are handled by Etsy. Etsy shares with us what it needs to deliver your order (for example your name and the personalisation you typed). We use it only to fulfil and support your order.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Etsy's own privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.etsy.com/legal/privacy

Our websites

  • Our modeler web apps run in your browser. Designs you make stay on your device unless you download or send them to us.
  • If you sign in with Patreon, we receive your Patreon name, email and membership tier only to unlock the tools you paid for.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: message Valencia Holdings on Etsy.
