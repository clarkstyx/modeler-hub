## Description

```
PERSONALIZED PHONE STAND KEYCHAIN · 3D PRINT FILES
A pocket keychain that flips open into a phone stand, with a can tab lifter at the tail and a name on the side that you can change before printing.

★ DIGITAL DOWNLOAD ONLY ★
You are buying 3D printing files. No physical item will be shipped. You need a 3D printer (or a print service) to make it.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Bambu Studio 3MF project, SMALL and LARGE: colours already set (navy body, orange flap, white name) and a ready name box on the side. Open, retype the name, slice, print.
• STL files, SMALL and LARGE (BODY + FLAP): for any printer and slicer (OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, Cura and more).
• START-HERE PDF guide (7 pages, real screenshots): how to change the name in Bambu Studio, how to add a name to the STL in OrcaSlicer, print settings, assembly and FAQ.
• Fredoka font (free, SIL Open Font License) used for the name.

TWO SIZES
• SMALL: phone + case up to 12 mm thick. Folded 70.7 × 35 × 11.5 mm.
• LARGE: thick or rugged cases up to 13.5 mm. Folded 76.9 × 35 × 12.5 mm.
Opens to about 85°; holds the phone screen at about 67°.

FEATURES
• Folds flat on your keys, flips open into a stand
• Can tab lifter slot at the tail (no bottle opener)
• 5 mm keyring hole
• Flap clicks shut under the front hook
• Prints on its side with no supports; the flap snaps onto the hinge for good

HOW TO PERSONALIZE
Bambu Studio: click text_shape in the Objects list › click the Ta (Text shape) tool › type the name. Font, size (7 mm) and height (0.6 mm) are already set. About 9 letters fit on the Small at 7 mm; the Large has a little more room.
Other printers: open the BODY STL in your slicer and add text with its text tool (step-by-step for OrcaSlicer in the guide). One-colour printer? Pause at the first letter layer to swap filament, or print it plain.

GOOD TO KNOW
• In the Bambu file the body and flap sit on separate plates, so the name needs just 1 colour swap.
• Slicer estimates on a Bambu Lab A1 (0.20 mm layers): body about 50 min, flap about 27 min. Your printer may differ.
• Suggested: PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 3 walls, 15–20 % infill, no supports.
• Names in the photos are examples. Photos are renders of the files; real colours depend on your filament.
• Because this is a digital item, Etsy does not allow returns. If anything doesn't work, message me and I'll help.

LICENCE
For personal use and gifts. Please do not share, resell or redistribute the files.

Designed by Valencia Modelers.
```
