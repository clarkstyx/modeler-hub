Sizes

Each charm is 20 mm tall, 9 mm thick (Q 24.8 mm with its tail), 11–25 mm wide. One 9 × 4.5 mm slot runs side to side and holds two 4 mm (550) paracords.

Print your letters

Bambu Studio (3MF)

1

File › Open Project… and pick Letters-A-Z.3mf or Numbers-0-9.3mf. Everything is on one plate with two filaments: 1 = body (purple), 2 = face (lilac).

2

Want other colours? Click a filament's colour square in Project Filaments and pick a new one.

3

Only need some letters? Delete the ones you don't need, or copy-paste (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V) letters you need twice, like the two Es in a name.

4

Slice plate, then print. Slicer estimate on a Bambu Lab A1: A–Z about 1 h 53 min, 40 g; 0–9 about 47 min, 14 g. Just one colour change, because the face is the top 1 mm.

Any other slicer (STL)

Import the STL files you need (OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer, Cura…). They already lie flat, face up. Don't turn them.

Two colours on a one-colour printer: in the sliced preview, add a pause / filament change at the first layer of the face (at about 8.0 mm height), swap filament and resume.

Suggested settings

SettingValue
OrientationFlat, face up (as the files open)
Supports / brimOff / off. The slot bridges by itself.

Walls / infill3 walls, 15–20 %
MaterialPLA (PETG works too)
BedAbout 65 °C for a full plate, so small letters stay down

Suggested starting settings. Every printer is a little different.

Good to know

Thin parts of a few letters (N, P, W) come close to the slot. You may see a tiny gap there; it doesn't affect stringing. If you prefer, print those letters with 4 walls.

String it & sell it

Make a keychain

1

Fold a length of 4 mm (550) paracord in half.

2

Push the loop end through each letter's slot, both strands side by side. Add spacer beads between letters if you like (not included).

3

Tie a knot (a snake or diamond knot looks great) above and below the name, and add a keyring or clip.

The cord is tight in the slot

Printed holes come out a little smaller than modelled. Clean the slot with a 4.5 mm drill bit by hand, or wrap tape around the cord's cut end so it slides in more easily.
