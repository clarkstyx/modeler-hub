How do I sign in on another device?

Press "Log in", type the email you paid with, and we send you a sign-in link. No password to remember.

How do I cancel or change plan?

Press "Manage" at the top of the page when you are signed in. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I sell what I print?

With the Seller plan, yes: you may sell physical prints made with any modeler, in any quantity, while your membership is active. You may not resell or share the exported files themselves. Other plans are for personal use.
