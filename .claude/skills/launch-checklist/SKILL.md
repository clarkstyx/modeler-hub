# Launch checklist

Run through this for every new modeler or big feature. Same content, four places.

## 1. MakerWorld (exposure)
- Post the OpenSCAD mirror as a Parametric Model Maker model. Defaults must look great in the auto thumbnail
  (a real name, two contrasting colours, nothing clipped).
- Title: what it is + "Generator" or "Customizable". Example: "Name Tag Generator (Customizable)".
- First line of the description: "Free single-colour version. Multi-colour 3MF, SVG upload and batch plates
  at <site>/m/<id>/". Then 3 bullets of what the sliders do, then print settings (0.2 mm, no supports, flat on the back).
- Licence: Standard Digital File Licence or CC BY-NC-ND. Commercial use is a Seller-tier perk on the site.
- Tag: parametric, customizable, keychain (or the category), Bambu, AMS.
- Add the MakerWorld URL to `modelers.json` → `makerworld` and redeploy so the card links to it.

## 2. Printables (free extra reach)
- Same files, same description, same link. Ten minutes.

## 3. Video (the real driver)
- 15 to 30 seconds, phone, vertical. Shot list: typing the name (2 s), one slider move (3 s), the 3D preview
  spinning (3 s), the slicer showing the colour parts (3 s), the printed part in hand (5 s), link on screen.
- Caption: "Type your name, print it tonight. Free version on MakerWorld, link in bio."
- Post to TikTok, Reels and Shorts the same day.

## 4. Patrons and email
- One short Patreon post: what's new, one picture, the link. Patrons who feel updates are frequent stay.
- Same text to the email list.

## 5. Reddit / Facebook
- r/BambuLab, r/3Dprinting, r/functionalprint (if functional), Etsy 3D-print seller groups.
- Lead with the print photo, mention the free version, answer every comment for the first day.

## After launch
- Note the date and the links in the modeler's README under "Where it's published".
- Watch two numbers for a week: MakerWorld downloads and new patrons. Nothing else.
