# Prompt template

Replace `[SUBJECT]`. Keep every other line as written.

```
A cute kawaii [SUBJECT] as a simple flat vector sticker icon, centered on a plain pure white background.
Flat solid colours only, no gradients, no shading, no shadows, no texture, no highlights.
Use at most 4 colours in total, including one dark outline colour (black or dark brown).
Thick, even, bold outline around every shape; all lines must be thick, no thin or hairline details.
Chunky, rounded, simplified shapes with no tiny details; small smiling face with dot eyes and rosy cheeks.
Everything connected into one solid silhouette, no floating or separate pieces.
Fills the frame, large and clear, no text, no border, no frame, square image.
```

## Extra line per set (append as the last line)

- **Christmas**: `Christmas colours only: one dark outline, red, green and cream.`
- **Everyday Cute**: `Soft colours only: one dark outline, cream, coral pink and teal.`
- **Line Art**: replace the "small smiling face" line with `One main fill colour with thick black line details drawn on top, like a bold marker drawing; small black or white accents for cheeks and highlights.`
- **Valencia tile**: replace the outline lines with `No outline; a flat rounded tile shape in one colour with a simple face drawn on it in one darker colour.`
- **No face** (objects like a candy cane or tree): drop the "small smiling face" phrase; keep the rest.
